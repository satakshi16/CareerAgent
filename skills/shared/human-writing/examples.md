# Before / After

Examples show style only. Every fact in a real draft must come from the master resume or career facts.

**Resume bullet**
- Before: Spearheaded a robust, cutting-edge NLP solution leveraging transformers to deliver transformative insights.
- After: **Cut manual policy review by 50%** by building a text classifier that flags insurance claims needing a second look.

**Summary sentence**
- Before: Results-driven data scientist passionate about leveraging AI in a fast-paced environment.
- After: Data scientist with 8+ years building ML and LLM systems for banking and insurance clients, from document agents to ticket routing.

**Em dash**
- Before: Built an evaluation pipeline — every change is benchmarked.
- After: Built an evaluation pipeline, so every change is benchmarked against ground truth.

**Cover letter opener**
- Before: I am thrilled to apply for this exciting opportunity.
- After: Your plan to automate claims intake with document agents is close to the work I lead at Acme Analytics, where my team built an agent that extracts line items from scanned invoices.

**Reflexive triple**
- Before: Fast, scalable, and reliable pipelines.
- After: An evaluation pipeline that benchmarks every change against a ground-truth dataset.
