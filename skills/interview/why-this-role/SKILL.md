# Why This Role (Connect, Context, Confirm)

1. **Connect:** name one specific, recent item about the company or team: a product, an initiative, a blog post or paper, a strategic move. Cite it in the doc with a date.
2. **Context:** link that item to a real project or result of the candidate's, and show an understanding of the business need behind it and where the business is heading.
3. **Confirm:** say plainly why this role is the logical next step and what the candidate would contribute.

Primary version: 45 to 75 seconds spoken (110 to 180 words). Short version: 2 to 3 sentences.

## Shape (illustration only; use real items and real projects)

> Connect: "I read about <Company>'s <initiative> in <month>, moving <process> onto <approach>."
> Context: "That's close to what I did at <employer>, where I built <project> that <result from sources>. The hard part was <real challenge>, and that seems to be where <initiative> is now."
> Confirm: "So this role is the next step for me: <what the candidate would own or contribute>."

No flattery ("I've always admired"), and no generic lines that would fit any company.
