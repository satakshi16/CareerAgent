# Hiring Manager Round

The manager asks: can this person solve my team's problems, work with my people and stakeholders, and explain their work to the business?

## Themes for the question bank

Past work deep-dives. Problem solving and judgment. Stakeholder management. Leading or mentoring. Working style and how the candidate works with the team. Questions about what the team is working on.

## Round section: `## Hiring manager essentials`

1. **Team problems I can solve.** 3 problems inferred from the JD and `company_brief.md` (mark inferences as guesses). Each one links to one real project from the resume.
2. **30/60/90 day outline.** 30: learn the systems, data, people, and current metrics. 60: one scoped win tied to a team problem. 90: own a workstream and propose the next bet. Keep it specific to this JD, and claim nothing about the company's internals as fact.
3. **Leadership and stakeholder stories.** Pointers to the 3 strongest stories (team size, conflict, influence without authority). Use real details only.
4. **Plain-language project stories.** For the 2 most relevant projects, a non-technical version following the "HM project story" rule in `explain-simply`.

## Study plan

Use `study-resources` on topics like the company's business model, the domain (for example banking risk or claims), and the product area. These are not technical refreshers.
