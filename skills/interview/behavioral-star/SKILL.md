# Behavioral STAR(R) Stories

## Round section: `## Behavioral stories`

Write 6 to 8 stories, labeled S1 to S8, each taken from a real resume bullet or career fact. Cite which one.

```markdown
### Story S1: <short name> (source: <employer, bullet or fact>)
- **Situation:** 1 to 2 sentences
- **Task:** what the candidate owned
- **Action:** 3 to 4 steps, "I" not "we"
- **Result:** the number from the sources, or [your result: ...]
- **Reflection:** what the candidate learned or would do differently
```

Each story runs 90 to 120 seconds spoken (about 220 to 300 words at most).

Then a map:

| Competency | Stories |
|---|---|
| Conflict | S2, S5 |

Cover: conflict, failure, ambiguity, influence without authority, leading a team, tight deadline, plus any competency the JD or the company's stated values name. Each story should cover 2 or more competencies.

## Rules

- A failure story needs a real failure. If the sources have none, use `[your example: a project that missed its goal]`. Never invent one.
- In the question bank, behavioral answers point to the story and give the opening line plus the angle to stress for that question.
