Stable preferences for every route and every run. Add a line here when the user states a lasting preference ("always", "from now on").

- The cover letter is shown in chat only. Never save it to a file.
- The candidate (John Doe, a sample person) is based in New Jersey, USA. Replace with your own name and location.
